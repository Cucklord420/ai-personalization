## Tools, maker equipment and resale

<a id="record-tool-ownership"></a>

- **tool-ownership** — Prefer durable, proven tools with an actual personal use; a multimeter has been more relevant than a soldering setup without a need. Do not assume expensive personal tools are available as department equipment.
  
  preferred; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10

<a id="record-tool-makers"></a>

- **tool-makers** — For a future 3D printer prioritize openness/local ownership, ease of learning, fine detail, reliable routine use, monitoring, repairability and low tinkering. No printer is confirmed owned; do not add filament/membership economics before a real printer/use exists.
  
  required; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10, S11

<a id="record-tool-resale"></a>

- **tool-resale** — For resale maximize net proceeds while balancing convenience, speed and shipping/return/scam risk. Prefer trusted low-hassle channels; compare buyback, individual sale and bundles using actual condition and current net offers.
  
  preferred; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10, S11

<a id="record-tool-psu-measure"></a>

- **tool-psu-measure** — For PSU/electrical-tool comparisons use original independent measurements/certifications suited to the load and use. A rated wattage, efficiency label or familiar brand is not a measured wall-power result.
  
  required; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10, S11

<a id="record-tool-wearables"></a>

- **tool-wearables** — For ring/wearable research, comfort, a thin normal-ring feel, comparable required features and full subscription TCO matter. A named comparison baseline does not prove ownership, best-in-class independent performance or a purchase decision. Smart-glasses fit requires the actual frame reference and a real fit test.
  
  preferred; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S11
