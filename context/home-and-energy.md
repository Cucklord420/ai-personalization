## Home, energy and resilience

<a id="record-home-plan"></a>

- **home-plan** — The current home is the prototype; a future home is a long-range plan. Prefer measured efficiency, local control, serviceability and resilience; no land, retrofit or equipment purchase is selected by this profile.
  
  preferred; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10, S11

<a id="record-home-light"></a>

- **home-light** — Prefer balanced natural daylight in a normal comfortable house, not a glass-box design. A basement mechanical/electrical/network service core and usable attic/bonus space were desired.
  
  preferred; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10, S11

<a id="record-home-sequence"></a>

- **home-sequence** — The saved planning sequence is network/security, measurement/energy audit, envelope/moisture work with ventilation, right-sized HVAC/electrification, then solar/battery decisions based on reduced measured loads.
  
  context; planned; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S11

<a id="record-home-engineering"></a>

- **home-engineering** — Heat pumps, ventilation/humidity control, accessible service routes, solar/battery readiness, critical loads and EV load management are design interests. Geothermal or radiant cooling require site/load/engineering/TCO evidence, not aesthetic preference.
  
  preferred; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10, S11

<a id="record-home-land"></a>

- **home-land** — A cheap parcel is not automatically good value: buildability, flood/wetland restrictions, utilities, site/geotechnical conditions, access, internet, taxes and builder feasibility must be checked before a land decision.
  
  required; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S11

<a id="record-home-solar"></a>

- **home-solar** — Portable solar/battery interest is for emergency/learning loads such as laptop, phone, router and lights. The saved plan separates it from permitted permanent household electrical work; do not assume backfeed, vehicle charging or free electricity.
  
  preferred; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S02, S11
