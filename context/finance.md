## Financial analysis preferences

<a id="record-finance-method"></a>

- **finance-method** — Use actual verified current information for financial analysis. Keep statement, current, pending, projected and posted balances separate; an unlinked or stale account is unknown/incomplete, not zero.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S13, S15

<a id="record-finance-reconcile"></a>

- **finance-reconcile** — Reconcile transfers, card payments, refunds and financing-plan conversions without double-counting economic spending or cash movement. A pending credit or scheduled payment is not a settled refund or completed payment.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S13, S15

<a id="record-finance-budget"></a>

- **finance-budget** — Prefer firm itemized weekly spending caps based on verified obligations, protected cash floors, buffer goals and deadlines. Distinguish a proposed example budget from a live reconciled budget.

  preferred; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10, S15

<a id="record-finance-cash"></a>

- **finance-cash** — Cash reserves should be liquid and FDIC/NCUA protected. Do not substitute an investment or assume inaccessible funds meet an immediate-cash requirement.

  required; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S02, S10

<a id="record-finance-investing"></a>

- **finance-investing** — For investment discussions, simplicity, automation, diversification and low ongoing fees are important; ETFs/mutual funds were preferred over stock picking. Historical dividend interest does not authorize trading or establish a current portfolio.

  preferred; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10

<a id="record-finance-card-value"></a>

- **finance-card-value** — For card comparisons prefer low/no annual fees and useful real redeemable value, with easy flat cashback or equivalent points favored over complicated redemption hurdles. Old card holdings and reward rates must be verified, not copied as current.

  preferred; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10

<a id="record-finance-promo"></a>

- **finance-promo** — Track exact promotion type, APR, deferred-interest terms, due dates, payment application and autopay evidence. Do not assume minimum autopay protects a promotion or turn old deadline notes into current obligations.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S13, S15

<a id="record-finance-priorities"></a>

- **finance-priorities** — For major spending, account for debt cleanup, protected reserves and sustainable cash flow before discretionary upgrades. Do not infer present affordability, balances, benefits eligibility or completed debt payoff from historical summaries.

  required; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10, S13, S15
