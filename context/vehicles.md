## EV leases and vehicle fit

<a id="record-ev-scope"></a>

- **ev-scope** — Research new battery EV leases only; a weak lease means wait, never substitute financing. The active Tesla shortlist is AWD-only and RWD is context, not a fallback or insurance proxy. The saved Muse EV watch applies AWD-only across its scope; keep that watch-specific gate when maintaining it.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: 10-17-2026; sources: S05, S06, S12

<a id="record-ev-features"></a>

- **ev-features** — Require ventilated front seats, smooth true one-pedal driving to a stop and native NACS/J3400 or an official supported adapter/access path. The current Car master treats under 250 EPA miles as a major downgrade, expects 250+ and prefers 300+; do not silently promote a lower-range near-miss.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: 10-17-2026; sources: S12

<a id="record-ev-size"></a>

- **ev-size** — Prefer compact/midsize vehicles; exclude three-row/full-size SUVs and large trucks. Comfortable seating, access, sightlines and ergonomics for a 6-foot-3 driver require a real fit check.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S02, S05

<a id="record-ev-software"></a>

- **ev-software** — Tesla is acceptable without CarPlay. Non-Tesla requires CarPlay unless Justin explicitly accepts unusually strong native software; GM/Chevy without CarPlay fails the standing rule.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S02, S05

<a id="record-ev-value"></a>

- **ev-value** — Toyota/Subaru/Lexus and luxury alternatives need unusually strong value. Apply all hard gates before considering a deal; do not stretch the spending cap for an attractive vehicle.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S02, S05

<a id="record-ev-cost"></a>

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S02, S05

<a id="record-ev-evidence"></a>

- **ev-evidence** — Require a current written exact-vehicle lease worksheet, matching direct-insurer quote and seating/visibility evidence before declaring a lease decision-ready. Verify affordability privately from current information.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S05

<a id="record-ev-impressions"></a>

- **ev-impressions** — Model 3 felt low/small during the test drive, but Justin accepts that feel for commuter use; do not reject it solely for low sedan packaging. Rear-seat room behind his driving position remains tight. Model Y height was preferred, IONIQ 5 access/height was difficult, and Mach-E felt stiff with screen interference and less-smooth one-pedal operation. These are subjective reported impressions, not current specifications or proof every fit requirement is met.

  context; reported; explicit/high; last supported: 10-18-2026; sources: S02, S05, S12, S17, S18

These are dated interests, not current stock, eligibility or buying recommendations.

<a id="record-ev-normalize"></a>

- **ev-normalize** — Normalize serious lease comparisons to 36 months, 10,000 miles/year and $0 voluntary cap-cost reduction. Separate required delivery cash from optional down payment and refundable deposits; a payment estimate is not a written lease disclosure.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: 10-17-2026; sources: S12

<a id="record-ev-precise-tco"></a>

- **ev-precise-tco** — Use the written Total of Payments, add only nonincluded required costs, and never count the first payment or tax twice. Quote identical-coverage insurance for the exact VIN/trim before ordering; RWD insurance is not an AWD proxy.

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: 10-17-2026; sources: S12

<a id="record-ev-no-old-law"></a>

  required; active; documented/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S12

  required; active; archive_explicit/high; last supported: unknown; sources: S10, S12
