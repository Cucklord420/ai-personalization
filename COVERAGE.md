## Review method

Full reachable text was ingested and fingerprinted before extraction. User turns were separated from assistant responses. Duplicate messages and truncated reader prefixes were reconciled with readable full-message supplements; explicit preference candidates were reviewed in context.

Current focused records resolved superseded architecture, EV timing, ownership and task-state claims. Historical questions were not all made permanent. Copied policies, assistant-written prompts, mixed-speaker/ambient voice text, sensitive records and obsolete market research were not blindly promoted.

## Remaining access limits

The entire current ChatGPT account outside the archived projects/targeted retrieval was not exposed as a complete corpus. Larger later live-capture/recovery ZIP assets returned no payload through the file route; alternate connector routes rejected binary decoding. They were not claimed read.

A large private employer-source release archive, every attachment/image, live device/account state, Muse reload, agent restoration, scheduler execution and notification delivery were not verified. The archive's historical completeness claim describes its capture scope/date, not a current account-wide export.

Raw chats, attachments, logs, financial/medical/contact records, proprietary SOP text and private source locators were not committed. Supported low-risk preferences and useful reported setup were retained; missing private/live values are explicitly unknown.
